Leslie Barrera - Portfolio

How to use
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

Files
index.html        Page content (edit your text here)
css/style.css     All styling, colors and animations (colors are in :root at the top)
js/main.js        Animations: name reveal, scroll effects, photo tilt, progress bar
images/hero.jpg   Photo in the top section
images/about.jpg  Photo in the About section

To publish for free: upload the whole folder to GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.
